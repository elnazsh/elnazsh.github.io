About

I am a research scientist working on speech and language AI, with around eight years of research experience and a focus on systems that have to run in production, in real time, for real users.

At DeepL I worked on DeepL Voice, a real-time speech-to-speech translation system for business communication. My role involved training and deploying the transcription models behind the system, building the audio and text data pipelines that supported model training, and developing large-scale evaluation frameworks to assess transcription and translation quality.

Before that I completed a PhD in quantitative linguistics at the University of Tübingen, in Harald Baayen's group as part of the ERC-funded WIDE project. My thesis, Wide Incremental Learning with Discrimination Networks for Auditory Comprehension, built computational models of how people understand spoken words directly from raw audio, grounded in error-driven learning. I hold an MSc in Computational Linguistics from the University of Stuttgart.
